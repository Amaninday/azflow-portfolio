Why custom development?

Use the tools you need. Don't automatically pay for the ones you don't.

A funnel stack can quickly become separate subscriptions for pages, forms, CRM, email, scheduling, integrations, and reporting.

Custom development gives us another option: keep the services that make sense, integrate what you already use, and build specific functionality where that produces a cleaner system.

Fewer unnecessary subscriptions
More control over the workflow
Functionality shaped around the business
Less dependence on one platform
This isn't “custom code versus SaaS.”

It's choosing the right architecture for the job. Hosting, databases, email delivery, APIs, security, and maintenance can still have costs.
